## Context

Spec-driven development frameworks ask you to write a specification first and
let it drive the implementation. Two of them — GitHub's SpecKit and Fission's
OpenSpec — looked worth understanding.

## Problem

Nobody asked for this. That is the honest framing: I wanted to know whether
these tools actually improve the output, or just add ceremony around work that
would have happened anyway.

A question like that is easy to answer badly. Read the documentation, form an
opinion, recommend the one with the better README.

## My role

A colleague and I each ran the trial independently. We compared conclusions
afterwards, found we had reached the same one, and presented it together.

## Approach

I used them on real work rather than on a toy problem — both frameworks, on the
Vue 2 to Vue 3 migrations I was doing anyway. A tool that helps on an invented
example and not on the actual codebase has told you nothing.

Running it twice, separately, was worth the duplication. Two people who like
the same tool for the same reasons is a weaker signal than two people who
arrive there without comparing notes first.

I compared them on the things that decide whether a team keeps using a tool
after the first week: how easy it is to navigate, how many artifacts it
generates, how much effort a review costs, and how verbose it is.

OpenSpec won on all four. Fewer artifacts, easier to navigate, easier to
review, less verbose — a better fit for small-to-medium tasks, which is most of
what we did.

## Outcome

We presented both to the team with the recommendation, including which one to
reach for and when.

The team did not adopt it — and that was the right outcome. Adoption has a real
cost: everyone has to learn it before anyone benefits. Against that, the
existing practice of Confluence pages, READMEs, runbooks and ADRs was already
adequate for the size of the projects we owned. Nobody could point to a
meaningful improvement it would buy us.

The team saw the potential and it stays on the table for larger work. A trial
that produces an honest "not yet" has still answered the question.

## Stack

OpenSpec, SpecKit, Vue 3.

## What I would do differently

DRAFT — approve this or replace it.

I evaluated on the work in front of me, which was small-to-medium and already
well understood. That is exactly the case where documentation practice is
adequate, so the result was close to decided before I started. To find out
whether these tools earn their cost, I would have to trial one on the work
where our existing documents actually fall short — something large, or
unfamiliar, or handed over from another team.

## Kontekst

Radni okviri za razvoj vođen specifikacijom traže da prvo napišeš
specifikaciju, koja onda vodi implementaciju. Dva takva — GitHub SpecKit i
Fission OpenSpec — delovala su vredno razumevanja.

## Problem

Niko to nije tražio. To je poštena postavka: htela sam da saznam da li ti alati
zaista poboljšavaju rezultat ili samo dodaju formu oko posla koji bi se ionako
uradio.

Na takvo pitanje lako je loše odgovoriti. Pročitaš dokumentaciju, stekneš
utisak i preporučiš onaj sa lepšim README fajlom.

## Moja uloga

Kolega i ja smo probu sproveli odvojeno, svako za sebe. Posle smo uporedili
zaključke, utvrdili da smo došli do istog i zajedno ga predstavili.

## Pristup

Koristila sam ih na stvarnom poslu, a ne na izmišljenom primeru — oba radna
okvira, na migracijama sa Vue 2 na Vue 3 koje sam ionako radila. Alat koji
pomaže na izmišljenom primeru, a ne i na stvarnom kodu, nije rekao ništa.

To što je proba rađena dvaput, odvojeno, vredelo je udvostručenog posla. Dvoje
ljudi kojima se isti alat sviđa iz istih razloga slabiji je pokazatelj od dvoje
koji do toga dođu bez prethodnog dogovaranja.

Poredila sam ih po onome što odlučuje da li će tim nastaviti da koristi alat
posle prve nedelje: koliko je lako snalaziti se u njemu, koliko artefakata
stvara, koliko košta pregled i koliko je opširan.

OpenSpec je bio bolji po sve četiri stavke. Manje artefakata, lakše snalaženje,
lakši pregled, manje opširnosti — bolji izbor za male i srednje zadatke, a to je
bio najveći deo našeg posla.

## Rezultat

Predstavili smo oba tima uz preporuku, uključujući i to kada koji posegnuti.

Tim ga nije usvojio — i to je bio ispravan ishod. Usvajanje ima stvarnu cenu:
svi moraju da ga nauče pre nego što iko od toga ima koristi. Nasuprot tome,
postojeća praksa sa Confluence stranicama, README fajlovima, runbook-ovima i
ADR dokumentima već je bila dovoljna za veličinu projekata koje smo održavali.
Niko nije mogao da ukaže na značajno poboljšanje koje bi nam to donelo.

Tim je video potencijal i to ostaje otvoreno za veće poslove. Proba koja daje
pošteno „ne još“ ipak je odgovorila na pitanje.

## Tehnologije

OpenSpec, SpecKit, Vue 3.

## Šta bih uradila drugačije

NACRT — odobri ovo ili zameni.

Procenjivala sam na poslu koji je bio preda mnom, a on je bio malog do srednjeg
obima i već dobro poznat. To je upravo slučaj u kojem je praksa dokumentovanja
dovoljna, pa je ishod bio skoro odlučen pre nego što sam počela. Da bih saznala
da li ti alati vrede svoje cene, morala bih da ih isprobam na poslu na kojem
naši postojeći dokumenti zaista ne pomažu — nečemu velikom, nepoznatom ili
preuzetom od drugog tima.
