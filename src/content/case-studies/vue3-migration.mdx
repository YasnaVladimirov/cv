## Context

Two applications running on Vue 2, both needing to move to Vue 3.

## Problem

The framework upgrade itself is well-documented work. The blocker was not the
framework.

Both applications depended on internal shared libraries, and one of them — a
component library owned by another team — had no Vue 3 release at all. An
application cannot move to a major version its dependencies refuse to follow,
and the fix was not mine to make. Waiting for the owning team would have parked
both migrations for an unknown length of time.

## My role

I ran both migrations.

## Approach

I treated each blocked dependency as its own decision rather than treating "the
dependencies" as one problem.

Where a Vue 3 version existed, I upgraded to it and adapted the calling code to
whatever had changed in its API.

Where none existed, I built a replacement inside the application — scoped to
exactly what that application used, and nothing more. Deliberately narrow: this
was a stopgap to be deleted once the owning team shipped their version, not a
fork to be maintained. Keeping it small is what makes removing it later cheap
rather than another migration.

That unblocked the work without waiting and without taking on somebody else's
library.

## Outcome

Both applications are live on Vue 3.

## Stack

Vue 3, Vue 2, TypeScript.

## What I would do differently

DRAFT — approve this or replace it.

The in-app replacement was the right call, but it is only temporary if somebody
actually removes it. I would tie it to something that will not be forgotten —
an issue on the owning team's release, or a note in the code that fails a check
once their version exists — rather than relying on the team remembering why it
is there.

## Kontekst

Dve aplikacije na Vue 2, obe su morale da pređu na Vue 3.

## Problem

Sama nadogradnja radnog okvira dobro je dokumentovan posao. Prepreka nije bio
radni okvir.

Obe aplikacije zavisile su od internih deljenih biblioteka, a jedna od njih —
biblioteka komponenti u vlasništvu drugog tima — uopšte nije imala izdanje za
Vue 3. Aplikacija ne može da pređe na veliku verziju koju njene zavisnosti ne
prate, a ispravka nije bila u mojim rukama. Čekanje na tim vlasnik zaustavilo bi
obe migracije na neodređeno vreme.

## Moja uloga

Sprovela sam obe migracije.

## Pristup

Svaku blokiranu zavisnost tretirala sam kao zasebnu odluku, umesto da „zavisnosti“
posmatram kao jedan problem.

Tamo gde je verzija za Vue 3 postojala, nadogradila sam je i prilagodila kod koji
je poziva onome što se promenilo u njenom API-ju.

Tamo gde je nije bilo, napravila sam zamenu unutar same aplikacije — ograničenu
tačno na ono što ta aplikacija koristi i ništa više. Namerno usko: to je bilo
privremeno rešenje koje treba obrisati čim tim vlasnik objavi svoju verziju, a
ne grana koju treba održavati. Upravo to što je malo čini njeno kasnije
uklanjanje jeftinim, umesto još jednom migracijom.

Time je posao odblokiran bez čekanja i bez preuzimanja tuđe biblioteke.

## Rezultat

Obe aplikacije su u produkciji na Vue 3.

## Tehnologije

Vue 3, Vue 2, TypeScript.

NACRT — odobri ovo ili zameni.

izdanje tima vlasnika, ili napomenu u kodu koja obara proveru čim njihova
verzija postoji — umesto da se oslanjam na to da će tim zapamtiti zašto je tu.
