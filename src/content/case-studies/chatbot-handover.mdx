## Context

A chatbot application inside a partner-facing web portal, letting business
partners change their account settings by typing what they want instead of
finding the right settings screen. Another team built it. It was moving to
mine, and I was the one responsible for the move.

## Problem

??? What actually made this hard? Pick the ones that are true:

- the code was spread across several repositories
- nobody on my team had run the service before
- we had no access to the services it depends on
- it was not documented
- something else

## My role

I owned the handover end to end. Not the original build — the transfer, and
everything the team needed to own it afterwards.

## Approach

Three things had to be true before my team could call it ours.

**The code had to live in one place.** It was spread across more than one
repository, so I consolidated it into ours.

**The team had to be able to run it.** That meant obtaining service access and
credentials, and submitting the changes that registered us with every system
the chatbot talks to. Unglamorous, and nothing works until it is done.

**The knowledge could not stay with one person.** I presented the system —
architecture, tools, knowledge base, prompt design, memory — to my team and
beyond it, because a handover that ends with one person who understands the
service is not finished.

Then I tested it, fixed what the transfer left behind, and prepared it for
release. One change I made along the way: the assistant used to confirm
actions by reading typed replies. I replaced that with explicit confirm and
cancel buttons, so intent could not be misread from free text.

## Stack

Python, TypeScript, React, and a hosted LLM provider.

## Kontekst

Chatbot aplikacija unutar veb portala za partnere, koja poslovnim partnerima
omogućava da podešavanja naloga menjaju tako što napišu šta žele, umesto da
traže odgovarajući ekran. Napravio ju je drugi tim. Prelazila je u moj, a ja
sam bila zadužena za taj prelazak.

## Problem

??? Šta je ovo zapravo činilo teškim? Izaberi ono što je tačno:

- kod je bio razbacan po više repozitorijuma
- niko u mom timu nije ranije pokretao servis
- nismo imali pristup servisima od kojih zavisi
- nije bilo dokumentacije
- nešto drugo

## Moja uloga

Vodila sam preuzimanje od početka do kraja. Ne originalnu izradu — prelazak, i
sve što je timu bilo potrebno da posle toga bude vlasnik.

## Pristup

Tri stvari su morale da budu ispunjene pre nego što tim to nazove svojim.

**Kod je morao da bude na jednom mestu.** Bio je razbacan po više
repozitorijuma, pa sam ga objedinila u naš.

**Tim je morao da može da ga pokrene.** To je značilo obezbeđivanje pristupa
servisima i pristupnih podataka, kao i izmene kojima smo registrovani u svakom
sistemu sa kojim chatbot komunicira. Nezahvalno, a ništa ne radi dok se ne
uradi.

**Znanje nije smelo da ostane kod jedne osobe.** Predstavila sam sistem —
arhitekturu, alate, bazu znanja, dizajn prompta, memoriju — svom timu i šire,
jer preuzimanje posle kojeg samo jedna osoba razume servis nije završeno.

Zatim sam ga testirala, ispravila ono što je prelazak ostavio i pripremila ga za
izdanje. Jedna izmena usput: asistent je ranije potvrđivao akcije čitanjem
otkucanih odgovora. Zamenila sam to izričitim dugmadima za potvrdu i
otkazivanje, da se namera ne bi pogrešno tumačila iz slobodnog teksta.
