## Context

Work went quiet. Rather than sit idle, the team decided to build something for
itself: one place to see error, warning and info logs across all of our
projects, and to assign a log to the developer who should deal with it.

Sentry and Datadog already do this. We were not trying to beat them. We had the
capacity, and a tool we understood end to end would be useful to us later.

That is the honest reason it existed, and it is worth saying out loud, because
it shaped what happened.

## Problem

Collecting logs is the easy half. The hard half is deciding what to show.

A log stream is more than anyone will read. Show all of it and it is noise, and
people stop opening the tool. Show too little and they stop trusting it. Every
useful decision in a tool like this is a decision about what to leave out.

## My role

I built the frontend. A colleague built the backend. I was in the architecture
discussions from the start — what belonged in the tool, what did not, how the
data should be presented, and what a log's detail view needs to carry.

That last question was the one we spent the most time on. A developer opening a
log has one decision to make: is this mine, and do I act on it. Everything on
that screen either helps with that or is in the way.

## Approach

The frontend was built around four things:

**Filters**, so the stream can be narrowed to what someone is actually
responsible for.

**Grouping**, so related entries are read as one thing rather than as a list.

**A detail view** carrying the information a developer needs to decide whether
to act — the part the team argued about most.

**Assignment**, so a log becomes someone's, and stops being everyone's.

I built it in React against the real backend as it came together, not against
mock data. Screens that have never met real data hide the problems that matter.

## Outcome

I left the company before it was finished, and it was never released.

What existed was a working React frontend running against a real backend:
filters, grouping, detail views and assignment, all clickable against real logs.
Not a prototype of screens — a partly built tool.

## Stack

React, JavaScript.

## What I would do differently

DRAFT — approve this or replace it.

We started from the tool we wanted to build rather than from the problem we
had. Nobody had written down what was actually going wrong with logs at that
point — which errors were being missed, and by whom. Spare capacity is a fine
reason to start something. It is not a definition of done, and without one it is
easy for a project to simply stop when the capacity does.

I would write down the question the tool has to answer, and for whom, before
deciding what it shows.

## Kontekst

Posla je bilo malo. Umesto da stojimo, tim je odlučio da napravi nešto za sebe:
jedno mesto na kojem se vide error, warning i info logovi sa svih naših
projekata i na kojem se log može dodeliti programeru koji treba da se time bavi.

Sentry i Datadog to već rade. Nismo pokušavali da ih nadmašimo. Imali smo
vremena, a alat koji razumemo od početka do kraja bio bi nam koristan kasnije.

To je pošten razlog zbog kojeg je nastao i vredi ga izgovoriti naglas, jer je
odredio sve što je usledilo.

## Problem

Prikupljanje logova je lakša polovina. Teža je odluka šta prikazati.

Tok logova je veći nego što će iko pročitati. Prikaži sve i to je šum, pa ljudi
prestanu da otvaraju alat. Prikaži premalo i prestanu da mu veruju. Svaka
korisna odluka u ovakvom alatu jeste odluka o tome šta izostaviti.

## Moja uloga

Radila sam frontend. Kolega je radio bekend. Bila sam u arhitektonskim razgovorima
od početka — šta pripada alatu, šta ne, kako podatke prikazati i šta detaljan
prikaz jednog loga mora da sadrži.

Na tom poslednjem pitanju proveli smo najviše vremena. Programer koji otvori log
ima jednu odluku: da li je ovo moje i da li nešto preduzimam. Sve na tom ekranu
ili pomaže toj odluci ili smeta.

## Pristup

Frontend je izgrađen oko četiri stvari:

**Filteri**, da bi se tok suzio na ono za šta je neko zaista odgovoran.

**Grupisanje**, da bi se povezani unosi čitali kao jedna stvar, a ne kao spisak.

**Detaljan prikaz**, sa informacijama potrebnim programeru da odluči da li da
reaguje — deo oko kojeg se tim najviše raspravljao.

**Dodeljivanje**, da bi log postao nečiji i prestao da bude svačiji.

Pravila sam ga u React-u uz pravi bekend kako je nastajao, a ne uz izmišljene
podatke. Ekrani koji nikada nisu videli prave podatke skrivaju upravo one
probleme koji su bitni.

## Rezultat

Otišla sam iz kompanije pre nego što je dovršen i nikada nije objavljen.

Ono što je postojalo bio je frontend u React-u koji radi uz pravi bekend:
filteri, grupisanje, detaljni prikazi i dodeljivanje, sve upotrebljivo nad
stvarnim logovima. Ne prototip ekrana, već delimično izgrađen alat.

## Tehnologije

React, JavaScript.

NACRT — odobri ovo ili zameni.

Krenuli smo od alata koji smo hteli da napravimo, a ne od problema koji smo

odlučivala šta prikazuje.
